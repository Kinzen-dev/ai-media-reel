The roll is one bar of 4/4, divided into sixteen equal steps. A hole is an enabled event; its width follows the stored velocity. Blank steps still keep their velocity for the next edit.

These are original patterns and simple synthesized voices. No samples, MIDI export or recording. Note and velocity editing inspired this small local workshop.
