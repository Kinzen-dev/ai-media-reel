Office

Tensile is an office of structural engineers in Rotterdam, founded in 1994 by Wietse Dalstra and Marit Oudekerk. We design structures whose first task is to span: footbridges, roofs over stations and markets, timber halls, canopies, towers and stairs. There are twenty-six of us. We have never designed a building with forty identical floors, and we do not intend to.

Architects call us early, usually with a site plan, a distance and a doubt. In the first two weeks we draw three load paths by hand, weigh each one in tonnes, weeks and euros, and argue for one. After that the model is shared: the architect works in the same file, and the geometry of every node is agreed before anyone draws a facade.

We detail what we design. Connection drawings leave the office at 1:5, the steel is weighed every Friday, and the engineer who signed the calculation is on site for the lift, the stressing and the load test. Since 2009 every footbridge we deliver is measured through its first winter and tuned afterwards if it needs it.

The ledger lists every work that left this office with a span worth stating. Sort it by any column. Each row opens to a short note and an elevation, and all elevations are drawn to one scale, so a stair and a harbour crossing can be compared honestly.
