Aldhelm Health had a comms room built for phones: one free slot, one 7.5 kW circuit and a 780 mm door. Rack 3 now serves the hospital's models, and nothing it reads leaves these walls.

Read the install notes

Corvid R1 is an inference appliance for organizations whose data cannot leave the building. Eight accelerator sleds and a control plane you run yourself, serving your models where the records already live, on hardware your own engineers can open, audit and seal.

DWG R1-02Sled, in layers

Sealed at the sled

Each sled boots only firmware signed for its home rack. Pulled from the chassis, it is inert.

DWG R1-03Rack of four

Fits the room you have

Four R1s fill a 42U rack at 38 kW. Front-to-back air drops into any hot-aisle layout.

DWG R1-04Three sites

Air gap is a setting

Roost federates sites over your own links, or none at all. Updates cross the gap on signed media.

Roost runs on the R1, not in our cloud.

The control plane ships inside the chassis. Scheduling, power, keys and model versions are managed from your network by your team, and nothing reports home.
