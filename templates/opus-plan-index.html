    Office

    Bureau HAV was founded in Ghent in 1998 by Lieve Halden, Joris Arntz and Mette Verhoeven. We number every work in the order it reaches the office, built or not, and we keep the numbers: there are now 412. Most of our buildings are a single room made precise, or a few rooms set inside a wall. We draw every project at the same scale before we draw anything else, because size is the first decision and the one most often left to chance; this page is that drawing. The office is eighteen people. We work for municipalities, schools, parishes and a small number of private clients, mostly in Flanders and the Netherlands, and we enter four or five competitions a year.
