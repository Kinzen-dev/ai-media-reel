Send us the artwork

Every drum is its own pass, and paper never lands in exactly the same place twice. We could chase the gap with pins. We keep it: that sliver of colour is how you know a sheet was printed.

The drum chart

Riso ink is translucent, so the second drum never covers the first: the two multiply. Every pair makes a third colour you can’t buy in a tin. Here are all twenty-five, overprinted the way the machine does it.

Paper

Uncoated, mostly recycled, every stock tested on the drum before it goes on this list. Riso ink dries by soaking into the fibre, so coated paper smears and we don’t carry it. Prices are per 100 A3 sheets. Bring your own and we’ll run a test sheet first, free.

The press

We publish five or six books a year, printed and sewn in this room, in editions that end. Titles first: the covers are on the shelf by the door, and they look better in your hands than on a screen.
