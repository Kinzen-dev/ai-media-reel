    The steel

    • A high-carbon Swedish tool steel, rolled in Värmland and bought by the bar.
    • Hardened in our own shop to 61 HRC, then tempered twice at 180 degrees.
    • Ground by hand on a wet stone, honed, and stropped before it hangs on the board.

    Hard enough to hold an edge through a day of oak, soft enough to sharpen on a stone at the bench.

    The handle

    Every handle is Swedish ash, felled in winter in Södermanland and split, not sawn, so the grain runs unbroken from ferrule to butt. The billets dry for three years in the loft above the forge before they are turned. Ash is springy where beech is brittle: it takes a mallet for fifty years without splitting. We finish it with raw linseed oil and nothing else, so it darkens and wears to your grip instead of ours.
