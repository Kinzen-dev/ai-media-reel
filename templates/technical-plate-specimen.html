What it sounds like on the way back

Most of a switch’s noise is made on the upstroke, when the stem slams into the top housing. We damp that seat with a 0.3 mm POM lip. Measured at 30 cm, the linear returns at 38 dB(A) and the tactile at 44. The click reaches 58, because the click is the point.

Eighty million strokes, still in spec

Every batch of 500 runs on the rig until a sample fails; none has before 80 million. Stems are moulded to plus or minus 0.02 mm, so the wobble of a cheap switch never starts. The footprint is the 14 mm plate standard: three pins, two pegs, no adapter.
